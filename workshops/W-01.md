# W-01 — Governance Lifecycle & the ICBC Authority Chain

## Learning Objectives

By the end of W-01, participants can:

1. Name the ICBC authority chain and explain why it cannot be bypassed.
2. Walk a governance artifact through the full lifecycle.
3. Explain why `REJECTED` is structurally terminal.
4. Distinguish *teaching examples* from *live* ratified artifacts.
5. Know where the live tools live (TEOS Forge engines).

## Agenda (90 min)

### Part 1 — Authority (20 min)

- What "sovereign" means: authority flows downward from the ICBC constitution.
- The authority chain (read from `docs/governance.md`).
- Rules that cannot be bypassed: downward-only authority, governance before
  automation, AI advises / humans decide, full audit.
- Why this is **not** a DAO.

### Part 2 — Lifecycle (30 min)

- States: `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → VALIDATION → COUNCIL_REVIEW → RATIFIED/REJECTED
  → DEPLOYMENT_READY → EXECUTED`.
- Transition table in TEOS Forge: `forge/src/proposal.js`.
- Structural guarantee: no transition out of `REJECTED`.
- Timelock: why RATIFIED is not immediately DEPLOYMENT_READY.

### Part 3 — First Proposal Walkthrough (30 min)

Participants trace a proposal using the template in
`examples/sample_proposal.md` and the tutorial in
`tutorials/proposal-submission.md`.

Steps covered:

1. Choose a proposal type and the matching council (scope routing).
2. Draft the artifact with evidence (template: `examples/proposal-membership.yaml`).
3. Submit → validate → council review (conceptual, using the engine contract in
   `examples/vote-recording.js`).
4. Decision: quorum + majority → RATIFIED or REJECTED.
5. Where each step is written in the audit chain.

> **Explicit note:** Nobody in this workshop is a council member. Every name
> used is a `<PLACEHOLDER>`. Completing W-01 grants **no** membership or
> authority. Membership comes only from a ratified onboarding decision.

### Part 4 — Lab & Verification (10 min)

Per the lab in `workshops/README.md`:

1. Read `docs/governance.md`.
2. Read the transition table in `forge/src/proposal.js`.
3. Verify: `canTransition("REJECTED", "EXECUTED") === false`.
4. List the audit events a rejected proposal leaves behind.
