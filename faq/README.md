# FAQ

Common questions about sovereignty, governance, and compliance in the TEOS
ecosystem.

## Sovereignty

**Q: What does "sovereign" mean here?**
Authority flows downward from the International Civic Blockchain Constitution.
No external party can grant or revoke governance authority; it is anchored,
constitutional, and non-bypassable. AI and code execute — they never govern.

**Q: Is this a DAO?**
No. There is no community voting, no permissionless protocol, and no token
vote. Governance is institutional and constitutional.

## Governance

**Q: What counts as a ratified decision?**
An artifact that passed structural validation, reached council quorum, met the
majority rule, and was recorded in the cryptographic audit chain.

**Q: Can a rejected proposal be executed later?**
No. `REJECTED` is structurally terminal and enforced by the Proposal Engine.

**Q: Who can be a council member?**
Only a person whose nomination was submitted with verifiable evidence and then
ratified. Nobody is a member "by default" or by assumption.
